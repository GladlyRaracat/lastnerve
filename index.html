The 2-week Reset

Pick one fight. Get a game plan and a trainer in your corner.

Bedtime. Mornings. Meltdowns. Screens. Sibling wars. Homework. Potty training. One target at a time, because that's what actually works.

  1. Before day 1 · 20 minutes

    Tell us what's happening

    Short online forms: your family, the behavior, when it happens, what you've tried. Then three days of quick tap-to-log notes on your phone.

  2. Day 1 · 45-minute video call

    Figure out why

    We look at your notes together and work out what the behavior is getting your kid. That "why" is what the whole plan is built on.

  3. Within 48 hours

    Get a written plan

    What to say, what to do, what to stop doing, and what to track. One page you can stick on the fridge.

  4. Days 3 to 13

    Run it, with backup

    Log each day in the app. Send up to three 2-minute videos of the hard moments and get voice-memo feedback.

  5. Day 14 · 30-minute video call

    See the graph

    Your before and after, in your own data. Then we decide together: done, next target, or keep going.
