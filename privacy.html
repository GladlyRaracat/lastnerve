Privacy notice

Effective October 12, 2026

Last Nerve collects only what it needs to run parent training. We never sell your information and never use it for advertising.

What we collect

WhereWhatWhy
Waitlist formName, email, stateTo tell you when spots open
Intake screening checkout (Stripe)Name, email, payment detailsTo take your $15 intake screening fee. Card numbers go to Stripe and are never seen or stored by us.
Intake, logs, video calls, and files (Google Workspace)Family details, your child's first name and age, behavior history, daily logs, videos you upload, messagesTo build and run your plan
Session transcripts (Krisp, only with your permission)A written transcript of your video callsTo help your trainer write your plan. Deleted after 30 days.
This websiteStandard technical data such as IP address and browser type, through our host (GitHub Pages) and font provider (Google Fonts)To load the page. We use no analytics or ad tracking.

Children's information

We collect information about children only from their parent or guardian, never from the child directly.

Who we share with

Only the service providers that run Last Nerve: Google Workspace for records, forms, files, and video calls; Krisp for session transcripts when you agree to them; and Stripe for payments. Each is bound to protect your data, and providers handling health information sign a Business Associate Agreement. We share with anyone else only with your written permission, or when the law requires it, such as a report of suspected child abuse.

How long we keep it

Records are kept for 7 years after services end, or longer if your state requires it for records about minors. Videos are deleted 30 days after your Reset ends. Waitlist entries are deleted on request.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of your records, ask us to correct them, or ask us to delete information we are not required to keep. Email kimmitchellpotter@gladlyrara.com and we will respond within 30 days.
