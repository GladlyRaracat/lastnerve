Intake screening

Every Reset starts with a $15 intake screening, and Kim reviews every intake personally. If your family is a fit, the $15 is credited to your first installment of the $249 founding Reset. If it isn't, the $15 is refunded in full within 5 business days, along with resources and referrals. If your family is a fit and you decide not to go ahead, the $15 isn't refunded.

Reset refunds

  • Cancel before the kickoff call: full refund, minus the $15 intake screening
  • Cancel after the kickoff call but before your written plan is delivered: 50% refund
  • After the written plan is delivered: no refund, because the core work is done

Founding families paying in 2 installments: refunds are figured on the $249 total. Cancel before kickoff, and your first installment is refunded, the $15 intake screening is kept, and the second installment is never charged. Cancel after kickoff but before your written plan, and the second installment is cancelled with nothing more owed. After the written plan is delivered, the second installment is still due.

Rescheduling and missed calls

Reschedule any call with 24 hours' notice at no cost. One courtesy reschedule per Reset is always free. A call missed without notice counts as completed.

Keep Going and Self-serve

Cancel anytime before your next billing date and you won't be charged again. Months already paid aren't refunded.

Not insurance, not a crisis service

Last Nerve is cash pay. We don't bill insurance or provide superbills. Parent training is a clinical behavior-analytic service delivered to the parent. In an emergency, call 911. In a crisis, call or text 988.
