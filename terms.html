Deposit

The $15 deposit holds a founding spot and comes off your first Reset payment. If the intake screen shows Last Nerve isn't the right fit, the full deposit is refunded within 5 business days. If you change your mind, the $15 holding fee is kept and isn't refunded.

Reset refunds

  • Cancel before the kickoff call: full refund, minus the $15 holding fee
  • Cancel after the kickoff call but before your written plan is delivered: 50% refund
  • After the written plan is delivered: no refund, because the core work is done

Founding families paying in 2 payments: refunds are figured on the $250 total. Cancel before kickoff, and your first payment is refunded, the $15 holding fee is kept, and the second payment is never charged. Cancel after kickoff but before your written plan, and the second payment is cancelled with nothing more owed. After the written plan is delivered, the second payment is still due.

Rescheduling and missed calls

Reschedule any call with 24 hours' notice at no cost. One courtesy reschedule per Reset is always free. A call missed without notice counts as completed.

Keep Going and Self-serve

Cancel anytime before your next billing date and you won't be charged again. Months already paid aren't refunded.

Not insurance, not a crisis service

Last Nerve is cash pay. We don't bill insurance or provide superbills. Parent training is a clinical behavior-analytic service delivered to the parent. In an emergency, call 911. In a crisis, call or text 988.

Content, scripts, and templates are for your family's personal use. Please don't resell or republish them. Our total liability is limited to the amount you paid in the last 3 months, except where the law doesn't allow that limit.
